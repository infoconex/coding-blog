**Topic:**  **ASP.NET MVC.**

**Abstract:** [ASP.NET MVC](http://www.asp.net/mvc) is a new web development framework from the [ASP.NET](http://asp.net/) team at Microsoft. In this talk, Brad Wilson will discuss what the Model-View-Controller pattern is, why the team decided to create a new framework, how it compares to [ASP.NET](http://asp.net/) WebForms, some of its primary benefits, and of course lots of time in Visual Studio walking through the uses of MVC.
 **Speaker: Brad Wilson**[Brad Wilson](http://bradwilson.typepad.com/) is a senior software developer at Microsoft on the [ASP.NET](http://asp.net/) team with more than 15 years of experience writing software for everything from networking stacks to high-speed measuring machines, from startups to large enterprises, and most everything in-between. He is currently working on the MVC team helping to define and deliver the next version of [ASP.NET](http://asp.net/) MVC. He is co-creator of the [xUnit.net](http://xunit.codeplex.com/) unit testing framework.
